🎉 Ferdinand
From Fredenandus , the Latinized form of a Gothic name composed of the elements friþus "peace" (or perhaps farþa "journey" ) and nanþa "boldness, daring" . The Visigoths brought the name to the Iberian Peninsula, where it entered into the royal families of Spain and Portugal.
